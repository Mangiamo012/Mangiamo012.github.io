Privacy Policy

Mangiamo: Italian Recipes · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mangiamo app ("the app", "we", "us") uses and why. In short: you do not need an account, we do not sell your data, and unless you choose to create an account, everything you do in the app stays on your own phone.

Who is responsible

The app is made and run by Nemanja Živanović, Požarevac, Serbia, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy.
Contact: appmangiamo@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Without an account, the following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your answers in the welcome screens (diet, foods you avoid, cooking level, servings, kitchen equipment) and the dishes you liked or skipped;
  • saved recipes, your weekly plan, your shopping list and the dishes you have cooked;
  • where you are in Cook mode, your reminder, read-aloud and measurement settings;
  • which dishes the Today screen showed you and which you opened in the last 14 days, so it does not keep suggesting the same ones.

Read aloud in Cook mode uses your phone's own voice; the text is not sent to us.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can also change it at any time in the app.

If you create an account (optional)

You can create an account with your email address and a password, so your recipes come back on another phone. Then we keep:

  • your email address and your password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form, so we cannot read it);
  • a copy of your saved and liked recipes, your week plan, your shopping list, your welcome answers (diet, foods you avoid, cooking level, servings, kitchen equipment) and the dishes you have cooked.

We use this only to keep your data in sync between your phones. It is stored with Supabase, protected so that only your account can read it. We never send you marketing email.

Foods you avoid. The foods you choose to avoid (for example gluten, eggs, dairy, nuts or sulphites) can show that you have an allergy or intolerance, which some laws treat as health information. Without an account this stays only on your phone. With an account, we store it only because you explicitly agree to it when you create the account, as the sign-up screen says. You can remove these choices in the app at any time (Profile → Food preferences & allergies), or delete your account; withdrawing your consent does not affect what was stored before.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account): your account and everything saved in it are deleted immediately. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account. Logging out or deleting your account does not delete the copy on your phone; use Profile → Delete all my data for that.

What the app loads from the internet

Recipes and photos are loaded from our recipe server, hosted by Supabase (Supabase Inc.). When your phone asks for them, the server receives the technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address and the type of device. We use this only to deliver the recipes and to keep the service working and secure. We do not use it to identify you and we do not build profiles.

App updates. When you open the app, it asks the update server of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA), the platform the app is built with, whether a small fix or new version of the app's content is ready. With that request Expo receives your IP address, the type of phone and app version, and a random installation code created by the app, so it can send the right update. It does not receive your name, email, recipes or tastes. We use this only to keep the app up to date and working.

Our website. These Privacy and Terms pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open them, GitHub receives the usual technical data of a web visit, such as your IP address.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example "Tonight: your planned dish") are created and scheduled on your phone itself. We do not send them from a server and we do not collect a notification address.

Purchases

Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription, or the one-time Lifetime purchase) is sold and charged by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. To know whether your Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives:

  • a customer code: without an account, a random code created on your phone; with an account, your account's code (a random ID, not your email or name), so your Premium also works when you log in on another phone;
  • your purchases as reported by the store: product, price, currency, store country, dates and renewal status;
  • technical data: your IP address, the type of phone, its system version and the app version.

We use this only to unlock Premium, restore it, and see how many people subscribe. RevenueCat does not get your email address from us. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment itself.

Emails you send us

If you write to us (for example from Profile → Send feedback or report a bug), we receive your email address, your message, and the app version and phone type that the feedback email includes. We use them only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

Ratings

The app may ask, through the phone's own system window, whether you would like to rate it. This is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive any personal information from it.

When you finish cooking a dish, you can tap whether it turned out well. Your answer helps the app suggest dishes you like, and it is also sent to us anonymously (only the recipe and the thumbs up or down, without any account, name or device identifier), so we can see which recipes cooks love.

Why we may use this information

Under the GDPR and similar laws, we rely on these legal bases:

  • technical data from requests to our recipe server, to the update server and to our website: our legitimate interest in delivering the recipes, keeping the app up to date and keeping the service working and secure;
  • your account and the data saved in it: needed to provide the account you asked for (performance of a contract);
  • foods you avoid saved in an account: your explicit consent, given when you create the account;
  • your Premium status and purchases: needed to provide the Premium you bought (performance of a contract);
  • anonymous thumbs up or down: our legitimate interest in knowing which recipes work well;
  • emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering you;
  • reminders: only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

What stays on your phone without an account is never sent to us, so we do not process it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library access.
  • No automated decisions about you with legal or similar effects.

Children

The app is a general cooking app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

How long we keep information

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app. Account data stays until you delete your account; if you ask us by email, we delete it within 30 days. Technical server logs kept by our hosting and update providers are deleted automatically after a short period under their own rules. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat, Apple and Google for as long as they need them for billing, tax and legal reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data about you, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to its use, withdraw a consent you gave, or complain to your data protection authority. In Serbia this is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs); in the EU it is the authority of the country where you live. Without an account there is nothing about you on our side; with one, you can see and change your data in the app and delete it there. For anything else, write to appmangiamo@gmail.com and we will help.

  • Supabase – recipe server, image storage and optional accounts
  • Apple App Store and Google Play – app distribution, payments, ratings
  • RevenueCat – checking Premium status and purchases
  • Expo (650 Industries) – app updates
  • GitHub Pages – hosting of this website

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They are bound by their own privacy terms and, where required, by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
