Privacy Policy

Mangiamo: Italian Recipes · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mangiamo app ("the app", "we", "us") uses and why. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or a password, we do not sell your data, and almost everything you do in the app stays on your own phone.

Who is responsible

The app is made and run by Nemanja Živanović, Požarevac, Serbia, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy.
Contact: appmangiamo@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

The app has no user accounts. The following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your answers in the welcome screens (diet, foods you avoid, cooking level, servings, kitchen equipment) and the dishes you liked or skipped;
  • saved recipes, your weekly plan, your shopping list and the dishes you have cooked;
  • where you are in Cook mode, and your reminder settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can also change it at any time in the app.

What the app loads from the internet

Recipes and photos are loaded from our recipe server, hosted by Supabase (Supabase Inc.). When your phone asks for them, the server receives the technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address and the type of device. We use this only to deliver the recipes and to keep the service working and secure. We do not use it to identify you and we do not build profiles.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example "Tonight: your planned dish") are created and scheduled on your phone itself. We do not send them from a server and we do not collect a notification address.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.), which receives an anonymous purchase identifier and the purchase status from the store. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Ratings

The app may ask, through the phone's own system window, whether you would like to rate it. This is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive any personal information from it.

Why we may use this information

Under the GDPR and similar laws, we rely on these legal bases:

  • technical data from requests to our recipe server: our legitimate interest in delivering the recipes and keeping the service working and secure;
  • your subscription status: it is needed to provide the Premium subscription you bought (performance of a contract);
  • reminders: only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

What stays on your phone is never sent to us, so we do not process it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Children

The app is a general cooking app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

How long we keep information

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app. Technical server logs kept by our hosting provider are deleted automatically after a short period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data about you, object to its use, or complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold accounts or profiles, in most cases there is nothing about you on our side; write to appmangiamo@gmail.com and we will help.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase – recipe server and image storage
  • Apple App Store and Google Play – app distribution, payments, ratings
  • RevenueCat – checking subscription status

These providers may process data outside your country. They are bound by their own privacy terms and, where required, by standard data protection safeguards.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: appmangiamo@gmail.com
