Delete your account

Mangiamo: Italian Recipes · by Nemanja Živanović

Accounts in Mangiamo are optional. If you created one, you can delete it and all data saved in it in two ways.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Mangiamo and go to Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything saved in it are deleted at once.

By email

If you no longer have the app, send an email to appmangiamo@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my account. We delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • your email address and password;
  • your saved and liked recipes, week plan, shopping list, welcome answers and cooking history stored in your account.

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. A copy that is still on your own phone stays there until you remove it in the app (Profile → Delete all my data) or delete the app. A Premium subscription is managed by Apple or Google and is not cancelled by deleting your account; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
